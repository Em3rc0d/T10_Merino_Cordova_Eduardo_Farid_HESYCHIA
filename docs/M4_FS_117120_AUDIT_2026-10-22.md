## Original and human review

The Freesound original WAV is accessed by signing in and clicking Download on the official sound page; see https://freesound.org/help/faq/ . The MP3 preview converted into PCM is NOT restored lossless audio.

Human review should document speech, music, traffic, second sirens/alarms, reverberation, compression artifacts, noise, and usable isolated interval. For contaminated sound, use pseudo-stem/contrast status, not a pure reference. A single isolated source still cannot satisfy train/val/test source diversity. No candidate row is falsely marked as accepted and no test partition is opened.

The .gitignore excludes local M4 audio and review outputs from commits.
